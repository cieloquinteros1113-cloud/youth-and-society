ABOUT THE PROJECT · 001

A place to
think out loud.

Youth & Society is an independent bilingual youth journal bringing together ideas, research, essays and perspectives on the world we share.

01 — OUR IDEA

Young people belong in the bigger conversations.

Conversations about politics, history, culture, society and international affairs often leave too little room for those who will also live with their consequences. Youth & Society begins with a simple conviction: age does not determine someone's ability to observe, research, question or argue.

02 — WHAT WE PUBLISH

Ideas with substance, not opinions for their own sake.

We publish essays, opinion pieces, cultural analysis, research and reflections on social and international affairs. We are interested in difficult questions, carefully built arguments and perspectives that bring something new to a conversation.

“You do not have to wait until adulthood to start thinking seriously about the world.”

03 — OUR OUTLOOK

Curiosity, judgment and responsibility.

Being a youth publication does not mean giving up rigor. We value research, clarity, intellectual honesty and respect for those who hold different perspectives. An argument can be firm while remaining open to evidence.

04 — OUR SCOPE

From Peru, toward an international conversation.

The project begins in Peru and aims to connect young voices from different places. We want the exchange of ideas to cross borders, languages and experiences without losing sight of the context each writer comes from.

WANT TO BE PART OF IT?

The conversation can start with you, too.

If you have a question, a piece of research or a perspective worth developing, you can read our editorial guidelines and send us a proposal.
