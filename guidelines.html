FOR CONTRIBUTORS

Submission Guidelines

A place for thoughtful ideas, original perspectives and conversations that deserve to be heard.

✳ Your perspective has a place here.
01 /

What we accept

Youth & Society welcomes original work exploring the social, political, cultural and intellectual questions shaping our world.

We consider essays, opinion pieces, research-based articles, cultural criticism, interviews and other forms of thoughtful writing.

Society Politics History Culture Education Philosophy International Affairs
02 /

What we look for

We value writing that demonstrates curiosity, independent thought and a genuine engagement with its subject.

  • A clear central argument or perspective.
  • Coherent structure and thoughtful development.
  • Reliable sources when factual claims require them.
  • Originality and an identifiable authorial voice.
  • Respectful engagement with differing perspectives.
03 /

Preparing your work

Before submitting, make sure your work includes:

  • A title.
  • Your name or preferred pen name.
  • A short author biography (optional).
  • The appropriate topic or category.
  • References or sources, where applicable.

Your work must be original and unpublished. If you used AI tools during the writing or research process, briefly disclose how they were used.

04 /

Editorial review

Every submission is reviewed by the editorial team. We may contact authors to discuss revisions, clarify information or request additional sources.

Submitting an article does not guarantee publication. Selected contributors will be contacted using the email address provided.

05 /

Respect and responsibility

We do not publish work that promotes harassment, discrimination, targeted abuse or deliberate misinformation. Criticism and disagreement are welcome when expressed with substance and respect.

Please do not include private information such as home addresses, phone numbers, school schedules or other details that could compromise someone's privacy.
